## 2. Research Goals & Objectives
1. Perform an exhaustive academic investigation into *The Last of Us Part II* (Naughty Dog, 2020) and its 2024 Remastered release.
2. Interrogate the mechanics of revenge, empathetic friction, perspective manipulation, de-optionalized violence, and moral agency.
3. Establish a dual narrative architecture (chronological fabula vs. presented syuzhet) analyzing information asymmetry and the midpoint protagonist reversal.
4. Execute full longitudinal psychological analysis of Ellie, Abby, Joel, Lev, Dina, Tommy, and supporting factions.
5. Investigate trauma, PTSD representation, and grief through clinical and ludonarrative frameworks without making clinical diagnostic claims about fictional entities.
6. Evaluate gender performativity, female physical violence, queer representation, and the moral service of marginalized characters.
7. Integrate ludic analysis: enemy AI naming, scent tracking, motion matching animation, and accessibility design.
8. Deliver an empirical synthesis of player polarization and critical reception.
9. Execute a full multi-stage academic pipeline (Research → Drafting → Gate 2.5 → 5-Seat Peer Review → Revision → Re-Review → Gate 4.5 → Final Publication Artifacts).
