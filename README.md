# The Last of Us Part II — Academic Research Project

**Project Title:** Playing Both Sides of Revenge: Perspective, Trauma, Empathy, and Moral Agency in *The Last of Us Part II*  
**Discipline:** Game Studies (Primary), Narrative Studies, Psychology of Interactive Media, Moral Philosophy, Trauma Studies, Gender & Queer Studies  
**Output:** Full Academic Research Manuscript (APA 7th, 8,000–10,000 words), Literature Synthesis, Multi-Perspective Peer Review, and Analytical Deliverables.  
**Framework:** Academic Research Skills (ARS v3.24.1)  
**Primary Object of Study:** *The Last of Us Part II* (Naughty Dog / Sony Interactive Entertainment, 2020), with comparative reference to *The Last of Us Part II Remastered* (2024).

## Project Overview

This project conducts a comprehensive, interdisciplinary academic investigation of Naughty Dog's *The Last of Us Part II* (2020). Moving beyond conventional reviews and plot summaries, this research examines how the game constructs characters, manipulates perspective and information distribution, structures trauma and grief, de-optionalizes interactive violence, and destabilizes player moral agency.
