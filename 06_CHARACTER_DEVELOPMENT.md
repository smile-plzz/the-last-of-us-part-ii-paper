# Longitudinal Character Development and Psychological Analysis

## 1. Theoretical Grounding of Character Analysis

Character analysis in *The Last of Us Part II* requires moving beyond static literary archetypes to examine characters as dynamic psychological entities embedded within extreme post-apocalyptic precarity. Drawing on **parasocial interaction theory** (Erb et al., 2021), **cognitive developmental psychology**, and **affect theory** (Ahmed, 2004), this analysis tracks characters not as moral exemplars or villains, but as deeply traumatized subjects caught in self-perpetuating cycles of retributive violence.

Special analytical focus is given to the interplay between **avowed ideology** (what characters claim to believe) and **enacted behavior** (what characters physically perform), highlighting the internal contradictions that drive the narrative tragedy.
